Mazda Occasion is the official certified pre-owned vehicle platform for Mazda France. Developed during my time at the digital agency NGA, the website allows customers to browse, filter, and reserve available cars across the entire national dealership network.

As part of the agency's development team, I was responsible for integrating the responsive interfaces, handling complex vehicle filtering layouts, and ensuring visual consistency according to Mazda’s strict corporate identity guidelines.
