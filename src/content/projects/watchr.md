Most movie tracking platforms have crowded interfaces and don't always combine movies, series, and animes into a single, clean tool. I wanted a simple, all-in-one tracker with a minimalist design to manage my watchlist without any visual noise.

Watchr is a Progressive Web App (PWA) built to solve this. It works like a custom to-do list for entertainment, allowing users to search and track the movies, series, and animes they have watched or want to watch. The project focuses on a clean layout and fast navigation.
