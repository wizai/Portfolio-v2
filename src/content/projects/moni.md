Launched as a modern alternative to traditional legacy services like Western Union, MONI (ex Monisnap) empowers diasporas worldwide to send money and make mobile top-ups to their families back home seamlessly. With millions of secure transfers processed to date, the platform removes friction to offer a fast, reliable, and user-friendly digital experience.

For 4 years, I worked as a Front-End Developer in the engineering team. My main focus was on building and maintaining user interfaces across three platforms: the transactional webapp and two marketing websites. I spent my time implementing UI designs, working on a global rebranding, and writing end-to-end tests.
